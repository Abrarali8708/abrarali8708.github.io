Privacy Policy – Anicq Bot

Last updated: November 2026

Anicq Bot is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the Anicq YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

The tool uses Google OAuth with the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other users.

Storage

The only stored credential is the channel owner's own access token, kept privately in encrypted secrets. No data is shared with third parties.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
